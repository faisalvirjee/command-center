🚨 Wash Sales

                
Disallowed losses for  · cross-broker scan with ±30 day window. HIGH = same-symbol stock buy. MEDIUM = STO put on same underlying (wheel-strategy).

                

                

                

                  About wash sales: The IRS disallows a loss when a "substantially identical" security is acquired within 30 days before or after the loss. Disallowed losses are added to the cost basis of the replacement security (not yet automated in The Forge — v2.3.x+). Selling a put option (CSP) on the same underlying as a stock loss can trigger a wash under IRS rules because it functions like maintaining the position. Option-on-option scan (v2.3.2a): Closing a put or call at a realized loss and selling a new option of the same type on the same underlying within ±30 days is also flagged. Whether two options with different strikes or expiries are "substantially identical" is interpretation-dependent — discuss with your CPA. Only realized losses (negative P&L) are scanned; profitable BTC trades are excluded by design. Disclaimer: This engine is informational, not tax advice — consult a CPA for filing.

The Forge is a single-file investment tracker that runs entirely in your browser. Your data lives in Google Drive — no server, no account required beyond Google OAuth.


                
First-time setup

                
    
                  
  1. 1
    Set a password — chosen on first run. Hashed locally with SHA-256. Forge never transmits it. If you forget it, use Forgot Password — your recovery key lives in Drive.
    2. 
                  
  2. 2
    Connect Google Drive — click the Drive status dot (top-right toolbar). OAuth opens in a popup. Once connected, Forge reads and writes forge_data.json to your Drive root.
    3. 
                  
  3. 3
    Import your first CSV — go to Import in the sidebar. Supported: Schwab transaction CSV, Fidelity transaction CSV. Forge categorizes every row, shows a preview, then waits for your commit. See Import & Reconciliation →
    4. 
                  
  4. 4
    Add API keys — go to Settings → API Keys. A free Finnhub key unlocks live VIX, economic calendar, news, and OHLC data throughout the app. See API Keys →
    5.

📥 Import & Reconciliation

                
Forge ingests broker CSVs and reconciles your reported cash balance against what it can derive from your transaction history.


                
Import flow

                
    
                  
  1. 1
    Download your transaction history CSV from Schwab or Fidelity. In Schwab: Accounts → History → Export. In Fidelity: Activity & Orders → Download.
    2. 
                  
  2. 2
    Drop the file in the Import tab. Forge parses every row and categorizes it into Stocks, Options, Dividends, or Interest. Unrecognized rows go to a Review Queue.
    3. 
                  
  3. 3
    Review the categorization preview. Deduplication runs automatically — rows already in your data are skipped.
    4. 
                  
  4. 4
    Click Commit. Forge writes the new rows, runs the reconciliation gate, and shows you the drift result.
    5. 
                


                
Understanding drift

                
After commit, Forge compares your reported cash balance (from the CSV) against the derived balance it reconstructs from your transaction history. The gap is called drift.

                

                  
StateDriftMeaning

                  
                    
✓ PERFECT$0.00Reported and derived balances match exactly.

                    
~ SUB-$1< $1.00Rounding or fractional shares. No action needed.

                    
⚠ AMBER$1 – $50Likely a wire, ADR fee, or unclassified event. Resolve via Review Queue or manual adjustment.

                    
✕ RED> $50Significant gap. Check for MoneyLink transfers, missing CSV date range, or special distributions.

                  
                


                
Resolving drift

                
Common causes: MoneyLink / wire transfers · ADR custody fees · special non-dividend distributions · reverse splits logged as "Other" · missing CSV date range.

                
    
                  
  1. A
    Review Queue first — unclassified rows from the CSV are held here. Open Settings → Review Queue, classify each row, and re-save. Correct classifications often close the gap entirely.
    2. 
                  
  2. B
    Manual adjustment — for events Forge will never model (wires, ADR fees). Open Settings → Cash & Reconcile → Manual Adjustments, add a labeled amount. Each adjustment reduces drift by that amount.
    3. 
                  
  3. C
    Re-import a wider date range — if your CSV doesn't cover all history back to your first trade, gaps appear. Re-export from the broker with a broader range and reimport. Deduplication prevents double-counting.
    4. 
                


                
Keep the seed pure. The canonical data in Drive is your source of truth. Never hand-edit forge_data.json directly — always use the Import flow so deduplication and reconciliation run correctly.

              


              
              

                
🧮 FIFO & Lot Engine

                
Forge tracks cost basis using FIFO (First In, First Out) — the IRS default method. Each buy creates an open lot. Each sell consumes the oldest lot(s) first.


                
How lots work

                
Example: You buy 10 NFLX at $96 (Apr 17), then 10 more at $92 (Apr 24). You sell 15 shares. FIFO consumes all 10 from the Apr 17 lot first, then 5 from the Apr 24 lot. The remaining 5 shares carry the Apr 24 cost basis.

                
Open lotA buy with shares not yet fully sold. Contributes to unrealized G/L on the Dashboard.

                
Closed lotA buy fully consumed by one or more sells. Contributes to realized G/L.

                
Partial sellA sell that consumes part of the oldest lot. Remaining shares stay open at the original cost.

                
Orphan rowA sell with no matching buy lot — usually from pre-history trades not in your CSV. Visible in Transactions with an orphan indicator.

                
History floorForge uses a rolling floor (last year or 2026, whichever is later) to avoid stale lot math from very old uncovered history.


                
Options lots

                
Each STO (sell-to-open) creates an open options position. A matching BTC (buy-to-close) or Expired event closes it. Assignment flows back into the stock lot engine at the strike price.


                
Wash sales: If you sell a stock at a loss and rebuy within 30 days, the IRS disallows the loss. Forge flags these in the Dashboard banner — they don't affect lot math but are shown for tax awareness.

              


              
              

                
💰 Cash Reconciliation

                
The Cash Reconciliation system keeps your reported broker cash balance aligned with what Forge can derive from your imported transaction history. Access it at Settings → Cash & Reconcile.


                
The four drift states

                

                  
BadgeThresholdRecommended action

                  
                    
✓ PERFECTExactly $0Nothing to do.

                    
~ <$1Under $1.00Rounding noise — safe to leave.

                    
⚠ AMBER$1 – $50Add a manual adjustment or classify Review Queue items.

                    
✕ REDOver $50Investigate before trusting Dashboard cash figures.

                  
                


                
Three resolution paths

                
Review QueueUnclassified rows held from the CSV. Classify them in Settings → Review Queue. Correct classification automatically reduces drift on next save.

                
Manual adjustmentFor events Forge will never model (wires, ADR fees, special distributions). Each labeled entry subtracts from the drift calculation.

                
Re-importWiden your CSV date range to cover history Forge hasn't seen. Deduplication prevents double-counting on re-import.


                
Reported balance

                
Forge reads your cash balance from the bottom of your Schwab CSV (the "Transactions Total" row). If your CSV is partial (date-filtered), the reported balance may not match what Forge can derive — this is expected and not a bug.


                
Copy queue for Claude — the clipboard button in Settings → Cash & Reconcile exports a structured summary of your drift, open queue items, and recent adjustments. Paste it into Claude to get a reconciliation diagnosis without sharing raw account data.

              


              
              

                
🎯 OS-Guidance

                
OS-Guidance (Options Selling Guidance) is a live decision surface for cash-secured put (CSP) trading. It answers two questions: Should I enter a trade today? and If yes, what structure?


                
How to use it

                
    
                  
  1. 1
    Pick a symbol from the watchlist pill row (SPY, PLTR, NVDA, NAIL, or any held position). Forge fetches live price, OHLC, VIX, and news.
    2. 
                  
  2. 2
    Pick a timeframe — 0DTE, EOD, 7DTE, 14-21DTE, or 30DTE. Each has different VIX and session thresholds.
    3. 
                  
  3. 3
    Read the Criteria Gate — all rows green = trade is clear. Any red row = specific reason to pause. Manual rows (⚠) require your confirmation (e.g. P/C ratio from Barchart).
    4. 
                  
  4. 4
    Read the Trade Structures — three tiers (Conservative 0.05Δ, Moderate 0.10Δ, Aggressive 0.20Δ) with estimated strike, premium, and credit shown.
    5. 
                  
  5. 5
    Check News & Sentiment — the sentiment banner gives a sizing hint (normal / reduce 30–50% / trade as-is). The gate decides IF. News modifies SIZE.
    6.

🔒 Data & Privacy

                
Forge is a zero-server app. Your financial data never leaves your browser session except to your own Google Drive.


                
Where your data lives

                
Google DriveAll persistent data is stored in forge_data.json in your Drive root. Only your Google account can access it.

                
sessionStorageYour session token (password hash) is cached here for the duration of your browser session. Cleared on tab close.

                
localStorageStores your password hash (SHA-256) and auto-logout timer preference only. No financial data is ever written here.

                
No serverForge has no backend. The HTML file itself is the entire app. API calls go directly from your browser to Yahoo Finance and Finnhub.


                
Pre-save backups

                
Before every save to Drive, Forge writes a timestamped backup file (default: last 5 days retained). If a save goes wrong, your previous data is recoverable from Drive. Manage backup retention in Settings → Data Management.


                
Exporting your data

                
Raw JSONDownload forge_data.json directly from Google Drive at any time — it is human-readable.

                
Transactions CSVUse the Transactions tab filter + sort to narrow the view. CSV export is a future backlog item.


                
Sharing with Claude: The "Copy queue for Claude" button in Cash Reconciliation is the intended way to share Forge data for analysis. Never paste your full forge_data.json into a public chat — it contains your complete trade history and cost basis.
